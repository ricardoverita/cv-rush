# Trademarks and branding

"CV Rush" is the project's provisional name. The name, logos, wordmarks and brand-identity artwork are **not** covered by the Apache-2.0 license (code) or the CC BY 4.0 license (content). See [ADR-010](docs/adr/ADR-010-licensing.md).

You may:

- refer to the project by its name to describe it accurately (for example, "built on CV Rush", "a fork of CV Rush");
- link to the official repository.

Please don't:

- use the name or logos for a fork or a derived product in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by it: give your fork its own name and visual identity;
- use the brand in a way that could mislead people about who operates a service.

If in doubt, open an issue and ask.
